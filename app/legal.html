Terms of use

What Styx is

Styx is a free tool that rewrites ArduPilot DataFlash .bin logs so they hold only the data you choose. It is written by Aditya Suresh (sasr-e24) and released under the MIT License. Using the app or the code means you accept these terms.

Styx is provided as is, without warranty of any kind. The author is not liable for any claim, damage or loss arising from its use, including data that is shared, lost, or wrongly kept or removed. The full wording is in the MIT License.

You check what you share

Styx removes what you switch off and keeps what you switch on. It cannot know what is sensitive for you. Positions, home location, flight times, text lines, and messages Styx does not recognise stay in the output if your selection keeps them. Open the output and review it before you send it to anyone. You are responsible for what you share.

Your logs and the law

Only process logs you are allowed to handle and share. Flight data can be subject to contracts, export control, data protection or other laws. Following those is your responsibility. Styx is not legal, export-control or security advice.

Privacy

Your logs never leave your computer

Logs are read and rewritten inside your browser. They are not uploaded, stored on a server, or sent to anyone. Output files are created in your browser and saved straight to your device.

No accounts, cookies or tracking

Styx has no accounts, sets no cookies, and runs no analytics. The page loads only its own files: no fonts, scripts or images from other sites.

What is stored on your device

Presets you save and your last selection are kept in your browser's local storage on your device, so they are there next time. They contain your choices, never log data. Clear your browser's site data to delete them.

Hosting

The app is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log visitor IP addresses for security. See the GitHub Privacy Statement. To avoid this, download the code and open app/index.html from your own computer; it works offline.
